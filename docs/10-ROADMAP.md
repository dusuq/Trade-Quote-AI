# 10 — 90-Day MVP Roadmap

Aligned to the blueprint's 3-phase plan. Detailed tasks live in `TASKS.md`; live status in `tracking/PROGRESS-TRACKER.csv`.

## Phase 1 — MVP Core (Weeks 1–4)

Auth · Multi-tenancy · CRM · Pricebook · Quote Builder · PDF Generation · Web app shell + design system.
**Exit criteria:** a contractor can log in, add a customer, build a line-item quote, and export a branded PDF.

## Phase 2 — AI + Delivery (Weeks 5–8)

AI Scope Generator · Voice-to-Quote · AI Copilot · Proposal Builder · E-Signature · Email & SMS delivery · Dashboard.
**Exit criteria:** a contractor can speak a job, get an AI-drafted quote, send a proposal, and collect a signature.

## Phase 3 — Mobile · Payments · Intelligence · Launch (Weeks 9–12)

Flutter apps · Offline mode · Payments · AI Vision · AI OCR · AI Sales Coach · Analytics · Integrations · Public launch.
**Exit criteria:** full mobile offline flow, online payments live, security review passed, public launch.

## Milestone Deliverables (per phase)

PRD · TDD · Architecture diagrams · DB schema · OpenAPI · Component specs · Test strategy · Deployment plan · Risk register · Production readiness checklist.

## KPIs to watch from day one

Quotes created/day · Avg quote completion time · Quote acceptance rate · MAU · AI usage/account · MRR · CAC · LTV · Churn · NPS.
