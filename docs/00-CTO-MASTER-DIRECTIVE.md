## TARGET CUSTOMERS

Electricians, HVAC, Plumbers, Solar, Roofers, Flooring, Landscapers, Painters, Cleaning, General Contractors. Business size: solo → 20 employees. Markets: USA, Canada, UK, Australia, NZ. Every UX/technical decision must reflect how these users work — **in trucks, on job sites, on mobile, with unreliable internet.**

---

## MARKET CONTEXT (validated)

- ~262k electricians, ~129k plumbers, ~120k HVAC firms in the US = **~510k+ target shops**; highly fragmented (no firm >5% share).
- Incumbents went upmarket: **ServiceTitan $245–500/tech/mo**, quote-gated, 12–36mo contracts, $5k–50k implementation, termination fees up to $39,375. Jobber ~$49–349/mo, Housecall Pro ~$59–299/mo — full FSM suites, not focused quoting.
- **The "missing middle":** a lean, mobile-first, flat **$29–79/mo** quoting layer for 2–10 person shops. Never copy competitors — build something **significantly simpler and more intelligent.**
