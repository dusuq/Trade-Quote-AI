- [x] `T201c` **[A1]** Scope the gateway's catch-all content-type parser — _deps: T201b_ ✅ **DONE (2026-08-02)** — **I did not take the audit's suggested fix, and the reason matters.** It proposed scoping the parser to the `/v1/ai` prefix; doing that would have silently broken **`POST /v1/pricebook/import`**, a _second_ multipart upload endpoint outside `/v1/ai` (pricebook-service's `import_csv`), capping every catalog import at 1 MiB. I grepped all 9 services before choosing: exactly two upload-bearing prefixes exist — `/v1/ai` (5 endpoints now, after T304/T305) and `/v1/pricebook` (1). So I took the audit's own stated alternative instead: keep a small global limit, raise it only where the upload lands. **Probed Fastify 5.10 rather than assuming the precedence rule** — a route-level `bodyLimit` overrides the parser-level one _unconditionally, in both directions_ (parser 4K + route 64K accepts 16K; parser 64K + route 4K rejects it).
