TradeQuote AI is an AI-powered, mobile-first estimating platform that enables electricians, plumbers, HVAC contractors, and other trade professionals to create accurate, branded, and customer-ready quotes in minutes. By focusing on the quoting workflow—rather than replacing an entire field service platform—it fills the underserved gap between generic proposal software and enterprise-grade FSM suites, giving small contractors an affordable way to win more jobs, reduce quoting errors, and grow their business.
